FAQ'S

1.Where can i register for COVID vaccination?

You can open the COWIN portal using the link www.cowin.in and click on "register/sign in".

2.Which age groups can register for vaccination on COWIN?

Those who have completed 12 years of age on the day of registration can register for vaccination.

3.Is online registration mandatory?

No,vaccination centers provide for a limited number of on-slot registrations per day.so,online registration is recommended for hustle free vaccination.

4.Is there any registration charge to be paid?

No,there is no registration charge.

5.How many members can be registered using same mobile number?

Up to 6 members can be registered using same mobile number.

6.Can i register without Aadhar card?

Yes,if you are above 18 years old you can register with any of the following proofs; a.Aadhar b.Driving license c.PAN card d.Passport e.Pension passbook f.NPR smart card g.Voter ID h.Unique disability identification card if you are 12 or above you can register with following proofs; a.Aadhar b.Unique disability identification card c.PAN card d.Passport e.Ration card with photo f.Student photo with ID card.
